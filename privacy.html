Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2025

Overview

OpportunityHub aggregates and displays job, internship, bursary, and graduate program opportunities as referral links. Applicants are redirected to the original platform (the "real platform") to apply. We do not collect or store applicant submissions beyond the listing metadata unless explicitly stated.

Data Stored

The site uses localStorage in the browser to store:

  • Posted opportunities metadata (title, company, type, description, referral link, deadline, etc.).
  • View counts approximated in localStorage (not centrally collected).
  • Verified email status (if user opted into account/email verification).

No sensitive personal data from applicants is stored on this site unless they voluntarily include information in a listing description (which is public).

Referral Links

All "Apply" buttons are referral links that navigate users to external platforms. OpportunityHub is not responsible for external sites' privacy practices. Please review the privacy policies of those platforms before submitting applications.

Cookies & Local Storage

We do not use cookies for tracking beyond what the browser uses for its standard operation. Opportunity data and basic counters are stored in browser localStorage. Clearing browser data will remove those stored items locally.

Ads

The site displays ads via Google AdSense. Google may collect information about your visit to deliver relevant ads; refer to Google's own privacy policy for details.

Children

This site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact the site administrator via the contact link on the site.
